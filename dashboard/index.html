How to read this
% followed up within 24 hrs = of workable inbounds with an exact submission time, the share that got any outreach (call, email or booked meeting) within 24 hours of submitting. Inbounds less than 24 hours old that haven’t been followed up yet aren’t counted until their 24 hours are up. Target: 80%.
Workable = inbounds minus unqualified. Needs follow-up now = workable inbounds with no outreach logged yet. Goal: 0. The Inbounds list below shows every inbound, including worked and unqualified ones, with a status that matches the Team view.

All inbounds

How to read this
Inbound = one person submitting one or more Sales-Intent forms on a given day (duplicates, tests and internal submissions removed).
The outcomes are a waterfall: every Meeting scheduled also counts as Connected, and every Connected also counts as Followed up.
Followed up = any outreach logged in Salesforce after the submission: an outbound call (any result), a manual sales email, an Outreach sequence email, or a booked meeting.
Connected = the rep reached the person: a call logged with an "Answered" or "Correct contact" result, or a booked meeting.
Meeting scheduled = a meeting (calendar invite) in Salesforce with this person that was booked after the submission (or up to 15 minutes before it, since booking-page meetings can sync to Salesforce before the form does) and not cancelled. Future meetings count. “Meeting booked for” shows the date of the first one.
Not followed up = no call, email or meeting logged after the submission. Percentages exclude unqualified inbounds.
Team = inbounds whose current Owner is on that team. Someone on two teams (e.g. Natasha) shows under both.
Unqualified = the Lead/Contact is marked Unqualified in Salesforce and wasn’t followed up. These are excluded from the follow-up rate so they don’t count against the rep (unqualified inbounds that were followed up still count as followed up).
Owner = the current Lead/Contact Owner in Salesforce (inactive users are marked “(inactive)”, queues “(queue)”). Unassigned = no owner, or owned by an integration/system user. Followed up by = the rep who logged the first follow-up.
Unqualified reason / by come from the Lead’s Unqualified Reason and Unqualified Explanation fields and its status history. Contacts don’t track these, so they show blank.
Hours to follow-up is shown only when the exact submission time is known; otherwise “Unknown”. Data refreshes each time the page is opened or reloaded.
